\begin{abstract}
Learned mesh-based simulators are trained with a pointwise regression loss and
therefore estimate a conditional mean. Whenever the conditional law of the
field given the model's observable inputs is broad---because the dynamics are
chaotic, because the forcing is stochastic, or, in the case we
emphasise, because a latent field governing the response is not visible to the
model at all---that mean is not a physically realisable state. We introduce two
mesh-native probabilistic hierarchical simulators, HI-MGN-V and cHI-MGNflow,
audit the benchmark landscape available to validate such models, and report
under a scoring protocol that is proper by construction.
\end{abstract}
